FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is AI MoneyGuard?+

AI MoneyGuard is a personal financial information and money-management tool that helps organise transactions, monitor spending and turn supported transaction information into useful financial insights.

Does AI MoneyGuard connect to my bank?+

MoneyGuard's Play Store description focuses on supported transaction messages and imported transaction data. It is not a bank, payment processor, lender, investment platform or money-transfer service.

Does it support multiple currencies?+

Yes. AI MoneyGuard is designed to recognise transaction information across multiple currencies and transaction-message formats.

Can I export my transactions?+

Yes. Version 3.3 includes CSV transaction export.

Is AI MoneyGuard financial advice?+

No. AI MoneyGuard is a personal money-management and information tool. It does not provide financial, investment, credit or lending advice.
